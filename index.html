About The Workshop

Join us at Restaurant Fish & Wine in the heart of Tbilisi for an unforgettable hands-on khinkali experience. Learn how to prepare traditional Georgian dumplings from scratch — from kneading the dough to perfectly folding 18 pleats.

Our workshops are intimate, warm and fun. You will enjoy local wine, learn cultural stories, and receive your official Khinkali Certificate of Achievement.

How It Works

1. Dough Preparation

Learn how to make traditional elastic dough using simple ingredients.

2. Filling Secrets

Prepare juicy meat filling with the perfect spice balance.

3. Folding Technique

Master the famous khinkali pleats and shaping technique.

4. Cooking & Tasting

Boil, serve, and enjoy your handmade khinkali with Georgian wine.
