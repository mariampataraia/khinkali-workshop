About The Workshop

Join us in a historic wine cellar in the heart of Tbilisi for an unforgettable hands-on khinkali experience. Discover how to prepare traditional Georgian dumplings from scratch — from kneading the dough to mastering their signature pleats

Our workshops are intimate, welcoming, and full of joy. You’ll enjoy local chacha and wine while hearing the cultural stories behind Georgia’s most beloved dish. Khinkali is not just food — it’s a living tradition.

How It Works

1. Dough Preparation

Learn how to make traditional elastic dough using simple ingredients.

2. Filling Secrets

Prepare juicy meat filling with the perfect spice balance.

3. Folding Technique

Master the famous khinkali pleats and shaping technique.

4. Cooking & Tasting

Boil, serve, and enjoy your handmade khinkali with Georgian wine.
